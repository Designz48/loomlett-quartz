ma good friend R came up with this insight when having run into the problem with procrastination. It turns out though, procrastination can be a helper. 

procrastination can actually be beneficial for writing your best work. Like how "people have a better memory for incomplete than complete tasks" "it stays active in our minds", and "ideas need time to mature" so "procrastination is one way to "restrain that urge to respond prematurely"". A great example is Abraham Lincoln not writing "the closing paragraph until the night before the speech before he finalized it." Leading him to give one of the most impactful speeches, the Gettysburg address, in US history. Not many would think this was done the last minute, having had 2 weeks to prepare!

This makes me realize that I'm not lazy after all.
