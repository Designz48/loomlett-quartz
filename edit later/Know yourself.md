#  how understanding perspective changed my life

_Why writing a book helped me to understand myself & know how to help others._

I used to draw art, from sketches to realistic portraiture on clip studio paint, I thought that I was pretty good.

In fact, though. I was only looking at my interests, but why was that? Because I was still learning about myself, I couldn’t look to what others thoughts were & what they would like to see- before I saw my own.

I knew that I could do realistic portraits because I could see things clearly, I had a bright imagination at 16 and I could transmit that to anything.

What i had was talent, but it wasn’t refined to suit a purpose. So I upgraded it, or rather, took it up a level.

I had been interested in 3D modeling in the past, so I used my talent of seeing objects clearly in my head- to the workspace.

I was a natural at sculpting, with practice I had my first realistic portrait on the computer! But there was still a lot I didn’t know.

Now, with my Graphic Design work, I can put my mind into anything- but most importantly, serve my audience.

_You need to know yourself first before knowing how to serve your audience._

Sure, you can put your heart & soul into a piece, but if it doesn’t help someone else- you were only helping yourself.

It’s tough to know your audience, but with time it becomes much more purposeful.

So that’s the first step, 

now~ take it to the **_Next Level!_**
