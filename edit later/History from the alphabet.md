_A little application of my studies in Graphic Design_

The Alphabet is synonymous of everything, isn’t it? 

There is the beginning of a road, the destination, and everything in between. 

Letters are the same. 

They are what we use every day, yet- many don’t know _how_ they were created.

---

One common fact is that they are from latin origin; they came from the Phoenicians but looked much different than now.

Then the Greeks took from them,

and then the Romans re-iderated them & added to it.

in mid-century to the 1800’s, the alphabet was further stylized to suit the technological advances.

Today- we have every & all styles of type at our fingertips! 

In fact, creating fonts and new typefaces has become more simple on the keyboard. Now, any & all typefaces can be created according to how diverse one’s imagination is! Though I admit personally, I’m still very bad at imagining new typefaces. I guess that’s why I’m in college… even though grabbing a typeface elsewhere could suffice just as well (except for the _I created it_ part).

That’s another thing though, distribution has become even more simpler.

When looking at a letter, in my designer mind I think, “How can I use this to communicate what I need?” and, “What visual qualities are needed to suit this?” 

For people writing and using the alphabet every day, what do you think?
