#### Conforming is to go against nature

- A businessman never accepting new ideas

It’s easy to feel comfortable in our house, with our computer, and in the same job. Often, change seems scary, yet we do it every day.

After a brutally hot summer, trees need to shed their leaves in fall else they’ll look terrible in spring instead of a vibrant, fresh green.

If you use the same skillet on the stove day after day, it’ll look worn out a lot quicker than if you switch it up.

It’s funny how after all the things that happen day after day that change, things like our routine, using that one object or thing for everything, or simply watching the same station…

Yet it’s deeper than that, if we don’t change our mentality we don’t grow. Instead, we keep aging without revitalizing our cells. In fact, when we learn new things that builds brain “fat”, if not, you know the saying — “Don’t use it, you lose it.”

There’s probably that one person you know who sticks to his/her opinion, doesn’t quite like any new idea you suggest, they seem the opitomy of the saying “old dogs don’t learn new tricks.” Though it doesn’t just apply with older people (sometimes not at all!), but with anyone of any age.

Think, “How am I taking this? Am I really considering if it’s a good thing or just sticking with my opinion?”
