**Today I started thinking** about [Words of Love (Numa Numa) by Different Heaven](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=75lVcnYl1ZQ&list=RD75lVcnYl1ZQ&start_radio=1). About 4-ish years ago when I first heard it, I had thought, *"This melody sounds so similar to something."* I went on a gut feeling, and found it! [My Life is a Party by Italo Brothers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HLgqP5LGBjc&list=RDHLgqP5LGBjc&start_radio=1), it's the same melody! Turns out, Different Heaven took the same notes for their own song- but made it better. Now, I'm a person who only really cares about the melody and sounds in a song, not so much the lyrics. Especially since most songs are often unoriginal since they rely heavily on love and heartbreak rather than, you know, other things. Though the lyrics in Numa Numa have always caught my ear, probably because of my love of color theory haha.

This explanation is gonna sound a lil' cheesy, but I'm just explaining since this is a medium where I can. *Boy this became a long update, sorry.*

Words of Love starts out with these lyrics, the main theme:

"When you leave my colors turn to grey
Every word of love I used to say
Now I paint them everyday."

After which it drops the beat. The following sounds are very interesting, because the original- My life is a party, was a *[[hardstyle]]* song, whereas Different Heaven made this to be, I think, Electro House. The lyrics are the next huge difference, because the original went:

"To... (bunch of places)
hello, hello, can you just feel it are you ready to go
do you receive my echo, my life is a party my house is a club..."

***See how superficial these lyrics are despite the awesome hardstyle sounds?*** Yet Different Heaven turned this around and made it *original*. The lyrics make sense, since a person can live all their life without knowing their "soulmate", or as the song by [Xillions](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jmn_E6TRtZQ&list=RDjmn_E6TRtZQ&start_radio=1) says it, "Somebody like me", or "what God has yoked together" as said in the [bible](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/mark/10/#v41010009); not knowing their lives are grey. Yet, similar to when someone finds their true purpose in life ([[In the Moment]]), their world lights up with color and they're invigorated again. I've seen it with one older person who lost their spouse, it's sad to think that the seemingly cliche "A part of my soul died with you," has some truth to it. A quote at the end of "The Art of Deception" by Bill Simon even says: 

>"I have this notion that there is a *right* person out there for everyone; it's just that some people aren't lucky enough ever to find their Mr. or Ms. Right. Others get lucky. I got lucky early enough in life to spend a good many years already with one of God's treasures... if I ever forget how lucky I am, I only need to pay attention to how many people seek and cherish her company."

On to my thought on color theory, the [[bright winter]] [[skin tone]] season is unlike any season because it's a clash of black on color. Like [DJ Pygme's song Lights](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Kp4nbndjd5k&list=RDKp4nbndjd5k&start_radio=1) *(watch until 20 secs in)* which I was actually inspired to write a short, unfinished, story about the idea *(idea from the last paragraph)* when I was around 16. While I think love stuff is cringy, I think I can feel this. So, the "colors turn to grey" reminds me much of bright winter because of this sudden contrast, I suppose. Though I feel I got on a lil' outta-da-way rabbit hole there.

Another song that's similar, is MUFFIN by CG5. It's always been a confusing one for me to understand since: a guy came out of the blue with it, the lyrics are sung by multiple people, and the lyrics themselves are... how do I explain it? So the lyrics include keeping the doctor away 'cause the love interest is an apple, in addition to the love interest being an angel food cake, and also the love interest being an ICEE. The sweet as candy reference makes sense, but again it all came out of the blue. 

These lyrics make this song original despite the theme of love being a readily overdone genre. I remember the interview with CG5 where he said something like, "I was walking and it came to me," which certainly makes for original music. I think what's interesting is the overall theme being the person knocking at the love interest's door, having known that person, and the love interest being an ICEE: cold? uncertain? hesitant? Sure isn't clear, but it reminds me of [No Go Go Go by 89ers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aVd86Q_ToPg&list=RDaVd86Q_ToPg&start_radio=1). I guess I can relate personally since I've never considered any relationship and anything about lovey-dovey stuff just weirded me out.

As for my personal experience with the song, it made a huge impact on me. This wasn't because I had been playing the guy's MC server for a month and was anticipating the song, I just didn't realize how it would affect me. It was the night before its release, and I was having the worst night of that year. Like, I was really upset. I'm a tuff gal though so I digress on details *(in which I've partly forgotten anyway :p)* and somehow I had my phone on me. 

So I go through YT and see the pre-release of Muffin, and when hearing it... idk why, but I start crying *(I'm a tough gal! Why did I divulge that!)* It's not like I was even listening to the lyrics, I guess it was just the melody. I don't even know if it was crying out of sad or happiness, but anyhow it's the 2nd most... alright fine, maybe it was the only song that made so much a difference to me from hearing it the first time. The other song is [Lonely World by K-391](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OlTE_58aJyA&list=RDOlTE_58aJyA&start_radio=1) for so many reasons, partly because it's remixed from [Summertime](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=25N1pdzvp4c&list=RD25N1pdzvp4c&start_radio=1) my fav melody in the world! Now however, I don't want to listen to either Muffin or Lonely World because of the feelings I get from them XD 

**Melodies tell a powerful story alone, lyrics often help make it more transparent.** Songs having a mixture of these can make deep impacts on people, as it has for me, whether it's how it resonates or it may even foretell someone's future. That last part was because of a YT comment I read where the girl was hearing a song that made her feel like searching, and then once she found it, she came back to the song to thank it, basically. Pretty interesting, right? After all, "I well know, O Jehovah, that man’s way does not belong to him. It does not belong to man who is walking even to direct his step."


Now I got [La Di Da by Marc Korn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=WmNA-1npzPo&list=RDWmNA-1npzPo&start_radio=1) in my head, the melody goes~ "all around the world it goes round and round and round~" *drop da beat*
