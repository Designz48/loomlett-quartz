A music producer from Chile, Riccardo played the piano and started making music about 20 years ago. Well-versed in [[Euphoric Hardstyle]], he's a smaller but well-known music producer who has been looked up to by many of the same genre. What many don't know, is that back before Alan Walker was known as "Alan Walker" he was named DJ Walkzz and was in a group of hardstyle creators like Kadenza and Pygme. While Alan Walker has never talked about his music producing career before Faded, there's facebook posts and YouTube comment interactions where he speaks highly of Pygme.

What's incredible about his music is the ability to mix genres and even completely different background sounds into a harmonious piece. Not many artists have mastered this amount of depth, and personally I liken every single one of Pygme's songs to Alan Walker's 100-hr-made music called Dreamer. Yes, I'm pretty sure Pygme has spent at least 20 hrs on each song, unlike other producers who focus on quantity rather than the quality. 

The music feels intelligent to listen to, like there's suspense and a new direction coming that can be learned from.
