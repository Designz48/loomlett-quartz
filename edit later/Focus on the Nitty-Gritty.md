#### It isn’t rocket science to do your best work

Whenever I see role models like Elon Musk, Steve Jobs, and other “successful” people, I wonder what their secret is.

The way they’ve become billionaires and have been dominating markets seems natural to them, like they’ve been chosen at birth to rule the world.

Now… don’t get me wrong, I don’t ever want to be a billionaire. But when seeing these people high on the pedestals of social media, it makes a person wonder.

While these people may seem gifted, it turns out that it may not be the case, I’ll explain why.

### Unless you’re born to a rich family…
