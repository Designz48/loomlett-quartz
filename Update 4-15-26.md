**Last night I had a funny experience:** I had cooked tuna steak on the stovetop, a salad w/ tuna stored in the microwave, and garlic bread in the airfrying. Usually I leave them as noted since investing in a hot plate to keep flies away and them warm might make more of a mess, and since I don't know when my Dad wants to eat, I just leave them as they are under cover. So when I told him all this was ready before I took my evening walk, I said in this order, "I have a salad in the microwave, tuna steak on the stove", "thank you!" he says, then I add, "and garlic bread in the airfryer." "Yay!" When I came back, he was complaining of a stomach ache, and so I check the microwave- the salad is still in there, so I show him the salad and he says, "I didn't know it was in there". By the way, he had the same kind of salad last night and said how tasty it was. I suppose he wanted garlic bread so badly though, that even though I told him *last* rather than first that there was garlic bread, he just saw "garlic bread, I want it." Unfortunately, I forgot to put a splash of [[ACV]] as I usually do, so no wonder it was heavy on his stomach... I was supposed to have had some too, but he ate all of it. He says, "Oh I'm sorry," and I say, "Sorry to you since it would've been better having the salad first than the others." All-in-all I'll remember that as soon as the garlic bread is done next time, I'll cut half and put away for myself so that he'll take a moment after finishing it to wonder where the dang salad is.
