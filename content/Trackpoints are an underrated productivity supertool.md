"TrackPoint offers several advantages over touchpads and regular mice. Firstly, it provides a more accurate and precise navigation experience, especially when working with fine details or editing documents. Additionally, it allows you to control the cursor without having to move your hands away from the keyboard, saving time and reducing strain on your wrists. Lastly, many users find TrackPoint's small size and discreet location appealing, as it doesn't take up much space on the laptop." -- Traitors against the trackpoint [Lenovo](https://www.lenovo.com/us/en/glossary/trackpoint/?orgRef=https%253A%252F%252Fwww.ecosia.org%252F&srsltid=AfmBOootEAWI3ohhxyjYHSxq-rCht4vf1DvNGqnVpQrD_0iGJ70P3tJ3)
