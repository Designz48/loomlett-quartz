# Boiling Frog Syndrome

If you intake a little bit of poison every day, over time it'll build-up, and you might gradually feel worse but not realize it, because of what's called the **[boiling frog syndrome](https://www.psychologytoday.com/us/blog/lessons-from-a-burnt-out-psychologist/202406/the-boiling-frog-syndrome-a-metaphor-for-burnout)**. There's a discomfort, certainly, but we may think the cause is something else if the root cause isn't apparent. 

For example, if you've ever watched Alfred Hitchcock, there's an episode where the maid wants to poison her employer *(I think)*, and so she puts a lil' [arsenic](https://www.fda.gov/food/environmental-contaminants-food/arsenic-food) into his coffee every morning. Over time, he feels a little less well, but doesn't notice it. Now, I don't really remember how it ends, but I believe the wife grew savyy about it- since it's easier to notice changes in someone from the outside rather than if you *were* that person, and is able to stop it.

> **Note**: [Arsenic](https://www.fda.gov/food/environmental-contaminants-food/arsenic-food) is an [increasingly apparent element in food](https://www.fondazionevalterlongo.org/en/longevity_articles/foods-that-contain-arsenic/) because of [pesticides](https://www.fda.gov/food/environmental-contaminants-food/arsenic-food). This includes rice, which needs to be soaked and then rinsed multiple times, [potato skins](https://sites.dartmouth.edu/arsenicandyou/arsenic-in-fruits-juices-and-vegetables/), [coffee and beer](https://www.fondazionevalterlongo.org/en/longevity_articles/foods-that-contain-arsenic/).
