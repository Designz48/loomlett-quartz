# How reality takes form 
Holding my hand up in front of me, pencil in other, I sketched along the outline and smudged in shadows. I had experimented with chibi art, didn't work. Anime, not great. Now, I was curious in sketching real life, and the drawing turned out exactly as my hand looked.

Having one's arm up is easy to draw since the image doesn't have to be imagined, and while it can be hard for those to sketch even shapes they see in front of their eyes- how much more so when it's all from imagination.

Yet, I succeeded in that feat too, because of the skill I learned in putting the image in my mind on paper.
