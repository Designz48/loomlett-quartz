# Chapter 1
## Layla

### It was Sunday, 8:43 PM

Layla hesitantly looked to her left. Out of the black limousine that was being driven, she could see the sunlight fade behind the trees darkened shadow. In front of where she’s seated is a lanky, sallow-faced man with a ball cap on his head, smoothly driving the limousine to the gray figure in the distance. Layla strained to see beyond the driver's hat, not sure of how she would make of the looming place she soon might call home.

A low, huffed voice began, "We've arrived at your destination, Miss."

"Thank you, Sir." Layla replied, grabbing her leather purse and getting out of the car, shutting the door behind. Careful that the slam wouldn't disturb the night's silence. After the driver got out of the car, he carried two blue suitcases out of the trunk and walked toward the front door- which was no more than twenty feet away. Layla trailed a few paces behind him as she eyed the building. Each step she took made her anticipate what was ahead of her...***A new future***.

---

The night air chilled. Even with my fleece-lined black zip hoodie, the strange excitement yet nervousness sent goose bumps along my arms. The driver went up the wide steps and stopped. Knocking precisely twice on the iron door, a butler came and greeted us. His face long and unemotional, taking my suitcases, he nodded for me to come. I trodded through the opening, warm air touching my face. 

*What an interesting smell*, I thought. Looking around, I noticed dark drapes covering windows on my right and left. Ahead of me, a purple carpet lay with a wooden door in front of it. The entryway didn't have much decor; white walls, table and cabinets underneath, the purple carpet so far being the only pleasant color I've seen.

Turning toward the door, I realized that I've been in a daze. The iron door was shut. *The butler must have closed it without my noticing.* Above the door was an Exit sign in LED lights. Turning again, the butler called, ‟This way please.” Walking down to the end of the carpet, he opened the door. I entered and closed it behind me, taking in the view ahead. The ceiling was considerably high, the room in general large and including modern-looking decor. In front of me is a spacious desk that extended in a circle with the middle being where *(I suppose)* the receptionist sat. She was middle-aged, a name tag stating Ann McMatthews.

‟Good evening‟, Ann greeted, her face neither apathetic nor emotionless. The butler started, ‟This is Miss Layla Alberts, she is the new trainee.‟
‟I see,‟ Ann stated, looking me over, ‟Good to have you. Mr. Brown will escort you to your room.‟ I nodded, looking over to the now-named Mr. Brown. Who- without a glance, walked through the hallway and up the staircase as I trailed behind him. The trim along the railing a white ivory. We climbed, maybe thirty steps before another hallway was before us. *It reminds me a lot like a hotel, but the rooms are a lot more closer together.* We continued walking until we stopped at room #503. The door white, as bland as the walls. Though something about the look of this place made it sleek and somehow vintage, like those mid-century modern houses I saw on a TV advertisement once.

Mr. Brown opened the door, a soft glow of light came on as I awed at the room. It was
quaint and simple, my own private room. No roommates or over-exaggerated designs of wallpaper, nor too small; *It's a perfect slate to start with!*

‟Bedtime is at 9:30, breakfast at 7. Good night.‟ Mr. Brown closed the door. I sat on the bed and opened my suitcases, spilling the different clothes on the bed. Breathing a sigh, I got ready for bed and settled to sleep.

His lips smaller with a subtle cupid’s bow, but his bottom lip slightly larger than the top. The trainee’s hair thick and having multiple layers that pushed to the left side of his face with the right side being neatly trimmed and sharp.
