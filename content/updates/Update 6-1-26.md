June 1st, no "June fools" today! Muahaa... the transition from bright green leaves to glossy green has already happened, but now they'll turn crispy. The shaggy hickory's at the forest edge, having all-day contact with the sun will start falling soon without their reminiscent transition to red, leading the air to have the slight feel of fall rather than the airyness of spring when its cooler out *(whenever that'll be)*. It's interesting how interconnected Summer and Autumn are, I've always considered the Spring colors to be closer to Summer but no- Spring takes on the brightness from Winter except that all is alive.
