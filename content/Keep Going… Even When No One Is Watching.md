## A message to the solopreneur living life on their own terms
￼
spenser-sembrat-hRXjYKQCFPE-unsplash
Goals are personal.

They represent us by our qualities, dreams, what we hope for, and do for.

Yet, people choose goals for different reasons.

They may be for our parents, friends, or someone else… so why are we doing it? Is it for their dreams or ours?

We can often feel like we’re under a magnifying glass.

￼
Whenever we’re at home, the park, running errands, these are obvious places to feel seen, but what about when you’re alone?

You have research piling up, analytics from your business to track, posts to schedule.

Who are you doing it for?

We can feel as if the whole world is watching us… even when we’re alone.

What if it was possible to be seen by just ourselves?

We know why we choose goals, and what we want in life.

That’s part of the reason we’re our own employer.

Keep Going ❤

— OJ
