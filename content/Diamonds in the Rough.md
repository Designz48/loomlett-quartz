## Freewriting those floating gems away.
![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*QLV_rjXWNk519eQDNj6ZWQ.png)

[Image generated with DALL-E](https://openai.com/dall-e-2)

**_Experiences stay with us._**

They’re like diamonds that form in the rough over time,

and once ready, they’re not easy to let go.

[](https://medium.com/plans?source=promotion_paragraph---post_body_banner_rabbit_hole_scribble--879bfd11e477---------------------------------------)

Yet, we have several of these diamonds floating around in our brain, cluttering space.

When our brain can’t organize for us, that’s where freewriting is the cure.

==Writing our thoughts down helps us not to forget things, to learn from an experience, keep note of a good experience we have.==

Not only do we have these **_diamonds_** in written form, but it becomes organized in our brain, too.

Writing is like manually sorting our brain, without having to dig in there to sort it ourselves (not like we humanly could anyway).

Some people call this journaling, others _freewriting…_
