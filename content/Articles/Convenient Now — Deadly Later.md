### All you can do with Windows you can do on Linux

At the 1/4 of a price, getting a brand new desktop with an AMD Radeon 6400 cost me $600, adding a 80Hz monitor on it was $100, and my performance is just fine. In fact, it’s better, because Linux doesn’t have what’s called “bloatware”, meaning programs run faster and with better efficiency. The countless “distros” — or versions of Linux, are also free.
