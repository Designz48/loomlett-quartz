We only have so much time.

Time for family, work, hobbies.

For all the goals that we’ve made for ourselves, only so many are actually accomplished.

And I mean **_really_** accomplished, not just done one part of.

In the mornings, or when you have a quiet moment, take the time to make note of all you’ve done, your limitations, and your goals.

This way, you can make note of them a week, month, or even years down the road and look back on them.

There, you can see how much you’ve learned in that time, and decide if you need improving on a topic in order to learn more.

Goals are there for a reason.

They’re nontangible in written form, yet mean so much when accomplished in our real life.

Plan for your growth now, and **_keep learning!_**
