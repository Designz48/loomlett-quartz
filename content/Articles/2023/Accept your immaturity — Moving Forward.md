_Graphic Designers are always scaling, but what about us who are just starting?_

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*F5FNXdNT5SIbImG9cajk4A.jpeg)

I say this from personal experience,

_“Being overwhelmed when getting started is the worst thing to do for motivation.”_

As a college student, there is so much to learn. But seemingly, there is also so little time to learn it!

At least, that’s how I feel when thinking of —

_what I need to do_ **VS** _what I can actually do._

From obligations in my personal life, to school expectations for when I need to complete a lesson, time _seems_ limited. But in reality, I have the whole world ahead of me!

Now that you know some background information of where I’m coming from, here are some strategies for how I’ve coped with this.

[ Note: Whether you’re someone who is in school or used to be, I hope this blog is helpful for those needing help & having been there. I’d appreciate it if you could share your knowledge :) ]

## **It starts with Mentality**

_“I can’t read everything”_

Yep, that’s right, OJ! You simply can’t.

I get Déjà vu whenever I say that, actually.

It’s a continual process of expanding my mind and then bringing myself back.

As **Karen Martin**, said “When everything is a priority, nothing is a priority.”

_What if I try out this hobby? Wait, I have to focus on this. Oh, you know what! I can create a template and sell it, I’m studying Typography in my college lesson so it’s a great way to apply it._

Some of the ideas I’ve come up with are great, because they:

- Open my mind
- Help my portfolio development
- Give me application for my studies
- Help me become knowledgeable in topics outside of school

But… I simply don’t have the **_experience_** to do so many things at once that I’d end up getting none of those things done!

I’ve read in many many online resources for side hustles that,

‘You need to reach out to as many social medias as possible!’

‘If you don’t use THIS resource, you’re going to fail,’

in reality- that resource, *side-hustle course, or membership, costs $30 a month!

Instead of falling into this trap, I simply think of **priorities**:

1. **_What_** I need to get done
2. **_Why_** does this need done
3. What to-do’s can I put on the backburner? Or… do I even **_need_** to do these at all?

**Often, I’ll put things that I _think_ I need done on my to-do list, but actually having no _need_ to do it.**

The next strategy shares the next step.

## _Scaling my Growth_

Taking **_Action._**

Doing something that you’ve never done before can be tough.

But they say, “Once you take the first step, everything becomes easier.”

Whoever said that meant that after you start:

1. You know _why_ you’re doing it.
2. You know _how_ you’re going to expend your efforts
3. You know that- it isn’t just about ‘knowing’ anymore; you’re **_doing_**!

Once you get in the routine, it’s easier to take a look at your situation and say, “Yeah, I got it.”

Even if you’ve never done it before…

don’t have the confidence…

or don’t have the experience (maturity) to start as strong as others miraculously seem to do-

it’s still a way of moving forward and giving it your best shot.

## Seeing your End-Goal

This applies to any of us, doesn’t it?

I was thinking the other day, _“When I finish college I’m going to land a freelancing job on Dribble that will make me a good amount of money.”_

In thinking that I realized:

_I’m going to have to set more goals in order to find clients._

So the process starts all over again…

1. **Change my Mentality** to work on what I need to.
2. **Scale my Growth** to take action and become experienced.
3. **See my End Goal** knowing that life keeps going, and that I have to keep pushing myself to reach new heights in my maturity.

**_Each of us have a journey that consists of transitions full of change and developments. If you like my journey please leave a follow! And come back next week to read a new story from me._**

*Not that these ‘side hustle’ resources aren’t bad, but relying ONLY on these does not give experience. But _acting_ on a game plan will.
