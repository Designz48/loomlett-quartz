Now: Why do I like to Write?

For all my life I’ve always had a natural inclination to grab a piece of paper and start writing about madly anything that came to mind.

And no, I never had a journal. In fact, I Hated journals at a young age.

So instead, I wrote stories.

Short stories… read stories…. I read 5, 500 pg books in 7th grade, that gives me some credibility, right? Then when I got older and entered high school, I wrote my own story. It amounted to about 200 pages.

You could say that I’m a natural storyteller, but when it comes to more “logical” concepts that aren’t as free-flowing, I really suffer at it.

That’s where Medium comes in.

As I research topics that I’m learning, writing about them helps me to understand them better.

Of course though, this isn’t just about me.
