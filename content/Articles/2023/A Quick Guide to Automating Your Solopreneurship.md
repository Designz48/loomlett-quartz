## Maximizing efficiency in your daily duties.
￼
Photo by Samson Katt from Pexels
Building a 1-person biz doesn’t mean you have to do it all on your own.
With new technologies like AI and other software, you can easily manage your endeavor without hiring anyone else.

This list will help any type of solopreneur, whether you’re a social media manager, video editor, graphic designer… the list could go on.

Here are my top picks.

1. Social media
Creating posts usually takes 2 steps: designing & editing.

Even if we aren’t a social media manager for some top-notch company, we still need to manage our own socials — and that’s tough!

Why do all the work manually when you can easily outsource to a freebie?

Predis.AI is an all-in-one for your promotion needs. With a click, you can generate endless reels and content for any platform- forever free! They have more freebies as well like a hashtag generator, video thumbnail picker, background changer, and lots more.
Ocoya is the same, including how it integrates with business platforms like Shopify, schedules posts, shortens links, and more!
Choose your pick between these 2 best social media graphic designers, as said by the Monday Roadmap.

2. Newsletter
For most of us, in the journey through our sales funnel… after social media… we need a newsletter for people to keep their interest.

However, with a good newsletter comes a lot of research, planning, and writing.

But let’s not make it difficult for our solo biz, shall we!

The Growth Bar SEO has some really incredible tools. It’s said to generate a whole newsletter from only an input of a few words, and be human-friendly, engaging, and converting! This free tool understands the best practices for a newsletter, and outputs what you need from it. Includes more tools also like giving you blog post ideas, rewrites your paragraph, and a research tool!
I was gonna try this myself, however, when I clicked the button it didn’t do anything. Hope you have better luck!

Next is Visme.co, an email newsletter generator just like the last one, except it also designs your graphics for you! Giving very aesthetic newsletter layouts and images, you can even add 3D graphics, customizable later, and data visualization charts to include.
If you want a more compiled list, this blog article from Rasa.io has more to check out.

Into the ground

or up in the air.

Helping you make accounting sales, simpler. This software is enterprise-grade and needs a demo for use, so check it out further and don’t take my word for it!
