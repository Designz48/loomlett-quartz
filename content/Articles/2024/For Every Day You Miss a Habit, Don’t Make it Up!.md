## Not everything has to be perfect

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*_rKqJFwAGCkNIm0IKGpIaw.png)

> Woke up today realizing it was 2 hours later in the day than you thought?

Whether you got to bed late, have an unscheduled appointment, or have some unplanned task to do — these can have the streak on your habit tracker crashing to the ground.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*WEZ2oj9_m_c5tmvliJZ2Nw.png)

Image from [Reddit](https://www.reddit.com/r/MicrosoftRewards/comments/1b3gx7a/anyone_else_lose_their_streak_today_0103_despite/?rdt=40629)

But you know what…

You don’t need to rush around finding 5 minutes of time to complete your habits, whether it’s:

- Reading
- Exercise
- A new hobby

Why?

Because when we rush to do it anyway, the quality of our work suffers.

Think,

> “What was the reason you started a habit in the first place?”

That simple question can help you a lot with your time management.

## What you can do instead

If you know that you absolutely need to get a habit done, take a breath and:

- Try it the next day
- Document your process
- Reward your tried effort

Not every day has to keep it’s _“perfect”_ streak.

Skipping a day won’t affect how you eat, sleep, or etc. **as much as long-term** **progress will.**
