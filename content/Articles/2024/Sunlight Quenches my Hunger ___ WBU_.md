## Planning a lil’ sunlight in your routine more often might do the trick

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*_rKqJFwAGCkNIm0IKGpIaw.png)

> Maybe it’s just me, but after sitting inside all morning and then wanting a snack, suddenly when I’m in the sun for an hour I don’t feel hungry anymore?

Especially after a big cup of coffee, even _with_ a snack, my hunger cravings weren’t going away. Then, when I realize I have to work outside for an hour, when I come back inside I don’t even notice that I’m hungry.

[](https://medium.com/plans?source=promotion_paragraph---post_body_banner_rabbit_hole_scribble--7cb42d6fa2c1---------------------------------------)

Is this proof that we, as humans, **_eat sunlight?_** Or is there another factor at play?

> Either the prior, or maybe my love of leaves as turned me into a leaf itself 🍃
