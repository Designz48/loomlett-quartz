## What intuition really means

> Have you ever felt a gut feeling urging you to take a shot?

Now tell me, how many times did those shots fail?

Your intuition isn’t just a cool superpower, it’s because your unconscious is working behind-the-scenes and giving you physical clues on what you should do.

As _Annie Murphy Paul_ says in her book **_The Extended Mind_**,

> ==_“_====The human cognitive system is **not equipped to handle** such tasks on the consciously controlled level_.”_

So no — you might not be a real superhero, but you can still use your ability to help others and yourself out.
