## ==If you think about pineapples, you probably are one🍍==

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*_rKqJFwAGCkNIm0IKGpIaw.png)

> What hobbies do you enjoy?  
> What did you watch last night?  
> Are you craving pineapple on pizza?

Each time you sit down, talk to a friend, and read a book you’re consuming information.

Often times, we gravitate to books and movies intuitively. Like picking the most gory cover on the shelf, or the movie that strikes our eyes the most.

Even with friendships, we feel out the person who _“gets us the most”_ on the first impression and ask for their number right away.

**_But are we really diving into what a person, movie, or book is about?_**

> “Insight perceives, intelligence understands, intellect comprehends, wisdom knows.” ― Matshona Dhliwayo

So tell me, is what you’re consuming beneficial for you? How will this help you down the road?

After all, every habit we build has not only a short-term, but a long-term effect. If you’re striving to be a different person, make note of everything you consume!

***You never know what might be the most life-changing.***
