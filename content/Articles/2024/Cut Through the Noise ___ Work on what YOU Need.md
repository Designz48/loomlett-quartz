## There’s no better way

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*_rKqJFwAGCkNIm0IKGpIaw.png)

Have you noticed when scouring through self-improvement articles, novels, or the etc. and actually try applying the tips & tricks…

> They don’t always work?

Said to be “tried and tested,” you’re sure that you’re just doing it wrong.

The truth is, even if a simple trick has been tested hundreds & even thousands of times by others, it doesn’t mean that it works for you —

Or rather, that the specific way they’re doing it will fit into your personal regimen.

## Cutting through

With countless shorts, articles, podcasts it can seem like an overwhelming wave of noise that’s static, never moving.

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*02VCu8efpIBgtZ0k3SslxA.jpeg)

Image by [cottonbro studio](https://www.pexels.com/@cottonbro/) from [Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/photo/man-in-brown-button-up-shirt-wearing-black-sunglasses-4842659/)

But there _is_ a way to cut through this endless wave so that each tip can be applied to you personally.

### ==Understand what you need==

- Your goals.
- What your passions are.
- How easily you get done your every day grind.

Organize these into its own place & figure out what’s working and not working in your day.

### ==Focu====s on you

When you filter what’s needed & not needed on your part, soon enough — those tips & tricks start to have meaning, but not in the _specific_ way an influencer said it.

> That tip get’s applied to your personal situation, helping you actually go somewhere!

## Takeaway

Making the focal point on yourself can help you apply any tip n’ trick to your situation, helping you move the needle in the direction you actually want.

By understanding yourself & keeping focused, cutting through the noise will be like a breeze~ _easy to do without feeling overwhelmed!_
