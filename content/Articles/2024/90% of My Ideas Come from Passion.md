## ==There’s no better way to write==

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*_rKqJFwAGCkNIm0IKGpIaw.png)

> When I wake up, I write.

Starting with ideas I’ve already jotted down, I freewrite my thoughts & order them into an outline, then adding the images & my favorite publication.

Ideas don’t come as soon as I sit down, though.

They come from thoughts over the day, simmering in my mind, taking shape when I go through situations, which turn into experiences that I further reflect to learn on.

As someone who is passionate about self-improvement, **ideas** of this topic twist & turn in my mind as I sit down for college, work, or reading…

> Then suddenly pop into mind, created in the moment.

At least they seem to be in the moment, though they’re not.

Everyday you have thoughts floating in mind, too. Whether you’re conscious of it or not, an idea may pop forth suddenly — causing you to run & write it down, but that ideas simmered there for a long while before coming to fruition.

The only way we can let our thoughts digest is when we’re:

> **1****. Consuming topics we’re passionate about.
> 
> **2****. Dispensing our thoughts onto a platform we’re comfortable with.

Personally, I’m happy that my platform is Medium. Given that I’m able to read & write anything I want while supporting & being supporting.

All of my ideas… ok maybe 90%, come from passion. If not, I wouldn’t have any thoughts to think during the day, would I?
