If you drink coffee, you might have heard the phrase,

> _“rise and grind, it’s coffee time!”_

You know, that knock-out phrase that gets you up from bed & motivates you to go _right_ to your coffee machine.

To be brutally honest… this motivation is stupid.

Now before you make any assumptions, here’s what I mean: **Coffee is not the main force that gives you the energy to get through your day.**


There, I said it — **_the unbelievable_**.

> But there are ways to use coffee to _sustain_ your energy:

1. _Exercising throughout the day._
2. _Eating a meal before my coffee & a snack during._
3. _Drinking coffee in the late morning — mid afternoon._

From personal experience, these have really helped me to not rely solely on coffee & to get motivation in other ways.

**_What about you?_**
