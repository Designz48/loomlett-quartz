Being alone isn’t what many claim it is, like having no one to relate with or nothing to do.

Unlike _loneliness_, it’s a treasure that can only be given to those appreciating the small things in life. Things that could never be understood in a group of people.

People enjoying to be alone are thought to be introverts, yet even extroverts can benefit from taking a moment away from their grind.

Being alone isn’t about longing, it’s about contentment.

> “True contentment is not having everything, but in being satisfied with everything you have.” — Oscar Wilde
