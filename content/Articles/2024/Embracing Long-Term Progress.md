## You have only one chance to learn about a topic.
Sure, if you’re in college, you may review the information before taking an exam, and then have to know it during the exam.

**_But most likely, after this time_**….

> you’ll never know about this topic again.

School rushes us, keeping us from learning at our pace to **_really_** absorb it.

After all, these are things we need to learn in order to be an adult and earn money, right?

So if you can, slow down.

Take an extra few hours early in the morning to ponder over what you’re learning and think,

> “How is this really useful to me?”

Then, when you take those small steps, you won’t need to hustle through it all at once — in turn, **_you’ll have a better chance of applying it._**
