## Little things truly add up — just how will yours?

![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*_rKqJFwAGCkNIm0IKGpIaw.png)

> ==Everything you do right now will have a huge impact on you in the future====.

Don’t believe me?

==Look at yourself in the reflection of the screen, every small wrinkle, pimple,== ==**_even the lack of it was caused by something you’ve done in your past._**==

The thing is, every action we make adds up — ==even in ways we don’t realize.==

==Even the cereal you may have eaten this morning has had a significant effect on your health, mood, and your thoughts over the course of your morning routine.==

Just tell me, what _is_ your routine every day? What daily tasks do you do that make you laugh, cry, or help you appreciate life?

==Taking note of even the smallest things can help to diagnose larger problems.==

> ==“====Great things are not done by impulse, but by a series of small things brought together====.” ~ Vincent Van Gogh.==

Just take a step at a time to uncover them.
