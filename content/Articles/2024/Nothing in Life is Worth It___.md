Getting up, having breakfast, checking social media, laughing with your friends…

> Where do you think you’ll be at the end of this?

10 years down the road when you’re a little older and (hopefully) wiser, will the effort be worth it?

## …unless you work for it!

Everyday when you discipline yourself in your habits, prioritizing what’s most important, and taking time out for passion projects will **_help you down the road be where you want to be._**

Every habit, task, and bit of free time adds up after all!

Whether you’re a solopreneur, simply side-hustling, or at your 9–5 24/7, work for what you want now — no matter how hard it is.

> Otherwise, what ==_is_== worth your energy in life?
