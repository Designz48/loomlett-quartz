## How to incorporate good habits & be on-top of your solo biz
![](https://miro.medium.com/v2/resize:fit:700/1*Lnqo1EYtv6yhFDT0CLwgSg.jpeg)

[pexels-keira-burton-6624317](https://www.pexels.com/photo/black-boy-screaming-in-room-6624317/)

Life is like a cup.

We have our daily activities that we do,  
our day-job we earn money from,  
and family we’re with.

Everyday we take a sip from our cup per every activity we do.

We sip consistently, continuing to fill the water in our cup little by little as we sip little by little, too.

But what happens when our cup overflows?

When activities pile up and fall by the wayside?

[](https://medium.com/plans?source=promotion_paragraph---post_body_banner_unlock_stories_scribble--ee22ce6967b7---------------------------------------)

**_We can’t drink water that’s spilled,_**

> can we?

Same as we cannot tidy up a mess if we have a hundred other things weighing on us:

- Our kid got sick
- Our boss wants us in the office
- Our website for our solo biz is running slow

==How do we find time & actually organize our routine,  
so that we can consistently drink water in our cup — instead of letting it overflow?==
