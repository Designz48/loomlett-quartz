The days that Snowflake *(sorry, if I say his name I think the gov will be eyeing my site)* talked about when the internet had the freedom to do anything. Sure, it didn't look pretty, but the websites then were functional and mirrored why computers and the internet were invented in the first-place: to problem-solve, creativity, and (input here) to join in *"combining communication and computing",* that quote comes from one of the first guys to invent the internet, [one of the early websites.](https://datatracker.ietf.org/doc/html/rfc2555#page-2) 

I discuss this more in a deep dive where I make notes on the book *Innovation by Walter Isaacson*, just because I'm curious. I'll be updating this deep dive, [[Early Computing]], over time. As a [[second brain]]. Also I've always loved rabbit holes, to go from one topic to the next and link them together, that's what's so cool about the Obsidian graph.
