About 6 months ago I planted a dill weed transplant from a local store. It's always easy to pick up a transplant rather than growing it yourself, and it was doing well through the hard freeze, until it wasn't. However, many plants grow back. So when I saw similar-textured leaves sprouting back, I thought it *must* be dill weed. At the time I had [[chickweed]] growing, and so I pruned it back to give the dill some space, and it kept growing. The only problem with it then, was it didn't *taste* like dill weed. When small yellow buds started growing on it then, I thought *what did I plant?!* And after taking it through a plant identifier app did I realize it was German Chamomile!

Now, I didn't think I planted any. But I must've since they're not native here. I didn't expect the taste to be anything different from the dried chamomile tea I drink, but oh boy is it phenomenal. I don't know how to describe it, the smell and taste is a new species of its own; it's not like when certain poisonous fruit blossoms smell like cherries, it's a completely new smell. Turns out, the more chamomile is harvested, the more it grows back. And it sure did! This photo is the best harvest I've had, and there's more plants growing!
