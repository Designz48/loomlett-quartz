Recently my dad talked about how he wanted to list his products on Amazon again, and I was all for it. Hence I started helping him list since I was used to listing on Ebay when I had it, however, there were so many problems. Part of it was because the products were listed before *(on the same account)* yet there was no transparency in anything and they kept demanding for proof of ownership of any brand created. Even so, I went through the steps and there were *still* automated errors. It all got so frustrating because then my dad started getting anxious about not getting a lot of orders this year, so he kept pushing me to finish the listings. When I had a problem, he would just sit with me to go through it... what's the point of having me do them when he's there the whole time? Obviously he knows better than me. So I quit. 

After which, he goes through the same steps as me, *finally* lists products *(and has some appreciation for my frustration)*, but later has an irredeemable error that can't be fixed because of the platform's lack of transparency *(said they would list insecticides, now they won't thanks to an FDA thing that they didn't make us aware of)*. Now he's a little less anxious, realizing that there's nothing he can do, it just makes me glad I quit so that he wouldn't have put all his anxiety on *me* since *I* was doing the work.

I have a way of understanding well ahead of time if something isn't going to work out. I knew it with various platforms I tried out, and its saved me both time and money. Even though my dad *thinks* he has experience, there's things happening on the internet that he can't ascertain, in which I somehow have an intuition for... leading me not wanting to list anything anymore :D Defeatist? Maybe, but I'd rather not toil for nothing.

None of this makes him a bad person, it's just that he lets his anxieties take over instead of realizing that he can't control everything. He piles on so many projects that the garage looks a mess, since he hasn't done the one basic step in all things: cleaning up while you go. Excuses can be made like having too much work to do, but cleaning up *while* you go saves much time in the end compared to cleaning up all at one time. And honestly, *somehow* we're in a good financial situation, and I'd say that's by continuing to work at what we can control and "[Commit your way to Jehovah; Rely on him, and he will act in your behalf.](https://www.jw.org/en/library/bible/study-bible/books/psalms/37/#v19037005)" After all, I've seen countless examples of this being true, that it's ludicrous to put all the burden upon ourselves when this system is out of control.
